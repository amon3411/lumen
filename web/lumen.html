Connect your lights

Vesta works with any light Home Assistant can control — Gosund, GHome, Philips Hue, WiZ, Kasa/Tapo, Govee, LIFX, Matter, Zigbee and 1,000+ more brands. Every light added in Home Assistant shows up here automatically.

Setup
  1. Gosund & GHome: remove the bulbs from the Gosund app and add them in the Smart Life app. Then in Home Assistant go to Settings → Devices & services → Add integration → Tuya and scan the QR code with Smart Life.
  2. Other brands from Google Home: in the Google Home app open Settings → Works with Google to see which brands you use. Add each one with Vesta's + Add light button (or in Home Assistant). Google Home keeps working alongside Vesta.
  3. Lamps on smart plugs or wall switches: turn on Include plugs & switches in Settings.
  4. Create a token: your Home Assistant profile → Security → Long-lived access tokens.
  5. Copy this file to Home Assistant's config/www/ folder and open http://YOUR-HA:8123/local/lumen.html. The hosted demo page can't reach your home network.
